Section 1

Introduction

1.1 Background

The Government of Maharashtra has announced the "पुण्यश्लोक अहिल्यादेवी होळकर शेतकरी कर्जमुक्ती योजना – 2026" for providing loan waiver benefits, One-Time Settlement (OTS) benefits and incentive benefits to eligible farmers across the State.

Section 2

Objectives

The primary objectives of the proposed platform are:

  • ✓ Provide real-time visibility of fund utilization under the scheme
  • ✓ Monitor beneficiary payment transactions
  • ✓ Facilitate end-to-end transaction reconciliation
  • ✓ Provide centralized dashboard for Government officials
  • ✓ Generate management reports and analytical insights
  • ✓ Enable exception management and transaction tracking
  • ✓ Ensure transparency, accountability and audit readiness
